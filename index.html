how it works

four steps. no hands on the wheel.

1

fees land

every creator fee this coin earns is claimed into the agent wallet. that wallet is public. that is the whole bankroll.

2

agent scans

it watches new post-migration coins and runs each one through the same checks: liquidity, holders, age, bundles.

3

trades by rules

5% of the bankroll per position, max 3 open. take profit, trailing stop and hard stop are fixed numbers, written below.

4

every trade posted

entry, size, exit and pnl go public as they happen. profits are not withdrawn. they stay in the tank.

    rules v0

    the rules the agent drives by.

    design intent for v0. they get a version number when they change, and the change is posted.

    rulenumberplainly
    bankroll100% of feesevery creator fee claimed goes into the agent wallet. nothing is taken out.
    open positionsmax 3never more than three coins held at once.
    position size5% of bankrolleach entry is five percent of what is in the tank at that moment.
    take profit+40%, sell halfat plus forty the agent sells half and lets the rest ride on the trailing stop.
    trailing stop15%once in profit, a drop of fifteen percent from the high closes the position.
    hard stop-20%minus twenty from entry closes it, no exceptions.
    liquidity> $30kpairs with less liquidity are skipped.
    holders> 200pairs with fewer holders are skipped.
    pair age> 10 minno trade inside the first ten minutes of a pair.
    bundles< 25%scanner check: if bundled wallets hold a quarter or more of supply, skip.
    score≥ 60scanner check: a composite of the checks above. under sixty, skip.
    postingevery tradeentry, size, exit and pnl are posted publicly as they happen.
